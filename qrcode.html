What is a QR Code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a two-dimensional barcode that stores data readable by smartphones and cameras. You can encode URLs, text, Wi-Fi credentials, and more into a scannable square.

How to generate a QR code

Enter your text or URL and the QR code updates instantly. The codes are generated locally in your browser and can be downloaded as images. Perfect for menus, business cards, tickets, and promotions.

QR code use cases

Restaurants use QR menus, marketers use QR links to track campaigns, and event organizers use them for tickets. A free QR generator makes creating scannable codes fast and private.

QR code FAQ

Are QR codes free? Yes, generating a static QR code is always free here. Is my data uploaded? No, everything runs in your browser. How long do QR codes last? Static codes never expire.
