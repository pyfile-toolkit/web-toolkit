Word Counter — Count Words Online for Free

This free online word counter counts words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, and paragraphs instantly as you type. It is 100% client-side: your text is processed entirely in your browser and is never sent to any server, so your writing stays private and work is never interrupted by network delays.

How to Use the Word Counter

Simply type or paste your text into the box. The word count, character count, and sentence count update in real time below the editor. Use the Copy button to grab your text, or Clear to start fresh. Because everything runs locally, it works offline and can handle very long documents without slowing down.

Why Word Count Matters

Word and character counts matter for essays with strict length limits, SEO blog posts optimized for search engines, tweets and social captions with hard character caps, and job applications or cover letters. Knowing your exact counts helps you stay within guidelines, hit headline-length sweet spots, and keep content concise and readable.

Word Counter FAQ

Does this count characters too? Yes — characters, characters without spaces, sentences, and paragraphs are all counted alongside words. Is my text stored anywhere? No. Everything is processed locally in your browser, and nothing is uploaded or saved. Can I use it for SEO? Absolutely — track title length, meta description length, and word count to optimize your content.
