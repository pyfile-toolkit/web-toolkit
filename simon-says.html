Free Simon Says Memory Game

This classic memory game tests and trains your short-term memory. Watch the growing sequence of colors, then tap them back in the same order. Each level adds one more step — the sequence never repeats itself exactly, so the challenge scales with you. The game tracks your best level in your browser and lets you share your run to challenge friends.

Why play a memory game?

Sequence-recall games like Simon train working memory, attention, and pattern recognition — the same skills used in coding, music, and learning. Short daily sessions measurably improve recall span. And it's a great icebreaker: who in your group can remember the longest sequence?

Simon Says FAQ

How does scoring work? You reach level N by correctly repeating the N-step sequence; your score is the highest level reached. Is there a time limit? No — take your time, accuracy matters more than speed. Does it save my data? Only your best level is stored locally in your browser. How do I challenge friends? Use the share button after a run to copy a message with your score and a link to the game.
