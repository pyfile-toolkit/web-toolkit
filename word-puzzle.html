Free Daily Word Puzzle — Wordle-style

Guess the secret 5-letter word in up to 6 tries — the same puzzle for everyone each day, so you can compare results. Green = correct letter & position, yellow = right letter, wrong spot, grey = not in the word. After each guess the tiles animate and the on-screen keyboard remembers what you've learned.

Why daily?

One puzzle a day builds a streak — and a habit. Our daily seed means every player gets the identical word, so sharing your result grid (🟩🟨⬛) turns words into a social game: can your friends beat your score today?

Word game FAQ

How many letters? Five, always. What if the same letter appears twice? Feedback follows the Wordle rules: tiles are revealed left to right with duplicates handled fairly. Does it save my progress? Only your streak and best streak are stored in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
