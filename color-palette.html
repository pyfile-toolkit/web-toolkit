What is a Color Palette Generator?

A color palette generator creates harmonious color schemes from colors that work well together. It is essential for designers building websites, apps, and brand identities.

How to use it

Pick a base color and the tool generates complementary, analogous, and triadic shades instantly. Copy the hex codes into your CSS, Figma, or design tool.

Why use a palette generator

Consistent, accessible color schemes improve design quality and user experience. A generator removes the guesswork of matching colors.
