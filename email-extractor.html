How it works

The tool matches standard email patterns (local@domain with a real top-level domain), removes duplicates, normalizes case and sorts the list alphabetically. By default it also skips addresses that look like image or asset filenames (e.g. logo.png or style.css@cdn false matches); toggle the checkbox to include them.

What is it for?

Extracting contacts from an exported CSV or HTML page, cleaning a mailing list, auditing logs or documentation for stray addresses, or moving contacts between tools. Everything runs locally — sensitive lists never leave your machine.

Is it private?

Yes — extraction happens entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or logged.
