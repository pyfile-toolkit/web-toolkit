Free Online Developer & Web Tools

Web Dev Toolkit is a collection of free online developer tools that run 100% in your browser — no installation, no accounts, no server uploads. Whether you need a JSON formatter, password generator, hash generator, color converter, case converter, diff checker, word counter, or a URL slug generator, everything works instantly and privately.

Because every tool is client-side, your data never leaves your device. Feed in sensitive code, private text, or passwords with confidence that nothing is transmitted or logged. Ideal for developers, designers, students, and anyone who wants fast, dependable utilities without ads or sign-ups.

Why Use Browser-Based Tools?

Browser-based tools load instantly, work offline, and never store your data on a remote server. Unlike installable applications, there is nothing to download, update, or configure — open a page and it just works. They are also cross-platform: the same tool runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS.

Private, Fast, and Always Free

Every utility here is free forever — no hidden walls for core features. Contributions via Ko-fi keep the project online, ad-light, and independent. Bookmark this page and come back whenever you need a quick, reliable tool you can trust.
