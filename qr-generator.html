QR Code Generator

Turn text, URLs, WiFi credentials or emails into a QR code in one click. Preview instantly, choose error-correction level, download as PNG. Everything runs locally in your browser.

What can I encode?

Any short text: a website URL, a wifi login (WIFI:T:WPA;S:MyNet;P:secret;;), an email (mailto:hi@example.com), a phone number, an event, or a plain message. Longer text produces a denser QR — keep it under a few hundred characters for reliable scanning.

Why error correction matters

A higher level (Q or H) makes the code scannable even when part of it is covered by a logo or damaged, at the cost of a busier pattern. Use L or M for plain printed codes, H for codes printed on curved or textured surfaces.

Is it private?

Yes. Generation happens entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded or logged.
