How to check if you actually type 50 WPM

Take a timed test: type for 30 or 60 seconds and divide characters by 5 to get words per minute. Accuracy matters — a fast but sloppy result is not a real 50 WPM. Our typing test measures both and does the maths for you.

How to get past 50 WPM

1. Stop looking at your hands

Touch typing is the single biggest lever. Every glance down costs a beat.

2. Type slower to type faster

Deliberate slow practice with high accuracy builds the muscle memory that speed later rides on.

3. Practise 15 minutes a day, not 2 hours once a week

Short daily sessions beat rare long ones.

4. Use real text

Practise on the kind of words you actually type, not only pangrams.

5. Watch accuracy, not speed, for the first month

Speed follows accuracy; chasing speed first caps both.
