What Is a Slug and Why Does It Matter for SEO?

A slug is the readable part of a URL after the domain — for example /how-to-make-coffee. Search engines use slugs to understand what a page is about, and users read them to decide whether a link is worth clicking. A good slug is short, descriptive, hyphenated, and lowercase, with stop words and special characters removed. This tool produces exactly that, so you get cleaner URLs that are easier to share, remember, and rank.

How to Use the Slug Generator

Type or paste your title into the box. The slug updates live below. Choose a separator (hyphen, underscore, or none) and toggle lowercase if you prefer to keep capitalization. When you are happy with the result, hit Copy. Because everything runs in your browser, your text is never uploaded and the tool works fully offline.

Slug Generator FAQ

Does the tool remove stop words? By default it keeps all meaningful words but strips punctuation and symbols — you can remove extra words yourself. Can I handle non-Latin text? Non-ASCII characters are transliterated where possible and dropped otherwise, keeping the slug clean. Is my text stored? No — generation is entirely client-side. Why should slugs be lowercase? Lowercase, hyphenated slugs avoid duplicate URLs and are the convention almost every site follows.
