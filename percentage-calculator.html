How to Calculate Percentages

What is X% of Y? Multiply Y by X and divide by 100, e.g. 15% of 200 = 200 × 0.15 = 30. X is what % of Y? Divide X by Y and multiply by 100, e.g. 30 is 15% of 200. Percent change measures the increase or decrease from an old value to a new one as a percentage. Add or subtract percent applies a percentage markup or discount to a base number.

Percentage Change Formula

Percent change = (new value − old value) ÷ old value × 100. A positive result is an increase, a negative result is a decrease. For example, if a price rises from $50 to $60, that's (60−50) ÷ 50 × 100 = 20% increase.
