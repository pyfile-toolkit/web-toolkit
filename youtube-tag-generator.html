What is a YouTube Tag Generator?

A YouTube tag generator helps creators build a full set of relevant tags for their videos. Instead of coming up with tags one by one, you type a topic and a few seed keywords, and the tool expands them into a copy-paste-ready list — including long-tail phrases that are easier to rank for.

Why use YouTube tags?

Tags give YouTube extra context about your video's content. Combined with a strong title and description, they help your video show up in search results and suggested videos. Tags matter most for new channels competing in crowded niches.

How to use this tool

  • Enter your video's main topic in the first field.
  • Add a few seed keywords separated by commas.
  • Click "Generate Tags" and copy the result into YouTube Studio → Video → Tags.

Pro tips

  • Include your exact title words as tags.
  • Add 2-3 word phrases, not just single words.
  • Re-check tags for trending topics in your niche.
