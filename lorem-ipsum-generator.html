What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem ipsum is scrambled Latin derived from Cicero's De finibus bonorum et malorum (45 BC). Typesetters adopted it in the 1500s as standard dummy text because its letter distributions look natural and — crucially — it doesn't distract readers with meaning. It remains the industry default for visual mockups.
